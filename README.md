# Task 4 – Comparative Study of Bioinformatics Databases

## Overview
This project focuses on a comparative study of three widely used bioinformatics databases: NCBI, UniProt, and the Protein Data Bank (PDB).

## Objective

The objective of this mini project is to understand the purpose, features, applications, advantages, and differences among these databases and their role in modern biological research.

## Databases Covered

- National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)
- UniProt
- Protein Data Bank (PDB)

## Repository Contents

- Mini_Project_Report.pdf
- Supporting screenshots
- README.md

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understood the importance of biological databases.
- Compared different types of biological information provided by each database.
- Learned the applications of sequence, protein, and structural databases in bioinformatics research.
